What is North Theory?

Clear Arctic knowledge for curious travellers.

North Theory breaks down life and travel in the Arctic into simple, useful lessons. From what to wear in Tromsø in January to how to avoid common winter mistakes, the goal is to help visitors feel prepared, safe and curious about the North.
